# Guides for readers

Статус: редакторские материалы для будущей публикации. Локальный просмотр не означает
публикацию на lexinform.pl; правовые даты проверки указаны в отдельных гайдах.

Russian texts written for the channel's readers, not for developers: what a reader can actually
do at each moment the bot tells them about, and how to do it well.

They live here as text while public publication remains an [open task](../roadmap.md).
When there is a site, these are what it is built from, and the cards get
short links to them («как подать?») in place of the paragraphs they carry today.

Rules for writing one:

- Every claim about the law names the act and the article, and says when it was checked. Where
  a practice is the ministries' habit rather than a rule, the text says so — the official form
  for a zgłoszenie zainteresowania lapsed with its delegation on 2026-08-28 and the ministries
  go on publishing the old one.
- Everything a reader must copy — a letter, a subject line, a list of what a submission must
  contain — is given as text they can copy, not described.
- Polish names of documents, offices and procedures stay Polish (the reader will have to type
  them into a form), with a Russian explanation the first time.
- No promises about outcomes. What a submission is *worth* is that it is published with the
  project's papers and has to be answered in the report — that is the whole of it.
